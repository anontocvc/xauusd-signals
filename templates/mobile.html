🔔 Push Notifications
Get notified instantly when a SNIPER or HIGH signal fires — even when your phone screen is off.
Works as a browser notification on Android. On iPhone: add to Home Screen first (see below).
📱 Add to Home Screen (iPhone)
Install this as an app on your iPhone — no App Store needed:
1
Open this page in Safari (must be Safari, not Chrome)
2
Tap the Share button (box with arrow) at the bottom
3
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4
Tap Add — the XAU icon appears on your home screen
5
Open from home screen — it runs in fullscreen like a native app
📱 Add to Home Screen (Android)
Install as a PWA (Progressive Web App):
1
Open this page in Chrome
2
Tap the 3-dot menu (⋮) in top-right corner
3
Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
4
Tap Install — opens like a native app from now on
🌐 Access from Anywhere (Ngrok)
Access your signal server from any network (mobile data, different WiFi) without a VPS:
1
On your laptop: python app.py
2
Open a 2nd terminal: ngrok http 5000
3
Copy the https://xxxx.ngrok-free.app URL
4
Open that URL on your phone — works on any network worldwide
